# Business Command Center

An all-in-one organizer built for modern small businesses juggling multiple lanes.

**Live App:** https://integrityrising101.github.io/business-command-center/

---

## Why I Built This

Running a modern business means managing multiple creative and operational tracks at once. Business Command Center keeps every lane in one fast, zero-friction view:

- 🤖 **AI Projects** — Track which tool you used (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Claude, Canva AI), prompts, and where the content goes.
- 📱 **Social & Website** — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Shopify, website updates, and email campaigns—all with due dates and priority.
- 👕 **Clothing Merch** — Drop planning, mockups, product descriptions, promo schedules, and launch checklists.

## How It Works

- **3-Stage Kanban Board:** Move tasks cleanly from **Planned** → **In Progress** → **Launched/Done**.
- **Category & Tool Search:** Filter by business lane or search instantly by AI tool, platform, or keyword.
- **Priority Indicators:** Color-coded dots (`high`, `medium`, `low`) so urgent tasks stand out immediately.
- **Focus Today Sidebar:** Surfaces what is due today or high-priority so you always know what to tackle next.
- **Zero Friction:** No backend, no login, and automatic browser `localStorage` persistence—just open and start organizing.

## Tech Stack

- **React + Vite**
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **GitHub Pages** (static hosting)
